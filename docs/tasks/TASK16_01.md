/skill:technical-documentation

Simplify docs/journal-plan.md.

WorldGL is a personal experimental project, not a formal research
or engineering organization.

The journal should document curiosity, experiments, failures,
discoveries and open questions in an informal, approachable tone.

Requirements:
- Prefer 4 sections: What I wanted to try, What happened,
  What I learned, What's next.
- Keep technical evidence and reproducibility details optional.
- Preserve the distinction between observations and hypotheses.
- Avoid academic language, bureaucracy and rigid templates.
- Keep entries short and enjoyable to write and read.
- Do not modify other files.
- Writing a journal entry should take less effort than performing the experiment it describes.
